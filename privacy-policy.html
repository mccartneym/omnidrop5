OmniDrop 5 Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

At a glance

  • OmniDrop 5 doesn't collect, store or share any personal information on any server.
  • Your games, scores, badges and settings stay on your phone.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Google Play handles the rating prompt and any purchase, under Google's own privacy policy.

Who we are

OmniDrop 5 is a game for Android in which you drop pieces in from any side of the board to make five in a row. In this policy, "we" means the developer of OmniDrop 5, and "the app" means OmniDrop 5. You can reach us at omnidropgame@gmail.com.

What the app keeps on your phone

To play, the app saves these things in its own storage on your phone:

  • the games you play, move by move, so you can resume a game and replay past ones
  • your scores, high scores and win streaks
  • the badges you've earned, your puzzle progress and your best in the mystery game
  • your settings, such as difficulty, board size, sound and the daily puzzle reminder

None of this is sent to us or anyone else. Other apps can't read it, and we can't see it.

Android backup

If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include the app's saved data in your device backup to your Google account, so your games and badges come back on a new phone. Google runs this backup. We don't have access to it. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to remind you once a day that a new daily puzzle is ready. The app asks before turning the reminder on, and you can say no or turn it off later in Settings.
  • Run at startup: to set the daily puzzle reminder again after your phone restarts. Nothing else runs.
  • Network access: Google Play's own libraries add this permission for the rating prompt and for purchases. The app itself doesn't send anything over the internet.

Google Play

After a win or a solved puzzle, the app may ask Google Play to show its prompt for rating the game. Google shows the prompt and handles any rating or review you leave. We don't learn whether you rated the game or what you wrote, except through the public reviews on Google Play.

If the app offers Premium for sale, Google Play processes the purchase. We never see your payment details. The app gets only a confirmation from Google Play that Premium was bought, and keeps it on your phone so Premium stays unlocked.

Google's handling of this information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Emailing us

The feedback button opens your own email app with a message addressed to us. Nothing is sent unless you send it. If you do, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to read and reply to your message. Ask us and we'll delete your emails.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Everything the app saves is on your phone, so you control it. You can swipe a game away on the High Scores screen, clear the app's storage in your phone's settings, or uninstall the app, which removes all of it. There's nothing for us to delete on our side, apart from any emails you've sent us.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we'll update this policy and its effective date above before that version of the app is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: omnidropgame@gmail.com
